EXPLORATORY

Everything on this page is exploratory. Targeted removals disconnect more sensory-to-motor pairs than random removals in raw counts, but none of the 24 targeted-versus-random comparisons survived Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction at alpha 0.05 (smallest raw p about 0.0476, smallest adjusted p about 0.0571). Treat these curves as leads for future work, not as findings.

Each curve shows the mean number of newly disconnected sensory-to-motor pairs as a fraction of nodes or edges is removed. Random removal is averaged over 20 replicates; targeted rules are single deterministic runs. Degree-matched random controls exist for some targeted runs (see below).
