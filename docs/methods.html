Methods and provenance

Data provenance

All analysis starts from MaleCNS v1.0, the complete male Drosophila central nervous system connectome, released 2026-06-08 under CC BY and queried from neuprint.janelia.org (dataset male-cns:v1.0).

Reference paper: Berg et al., "Sexual dimorphism in the complete Drosophila male central nervous system connectome", Cell 189(18), 2026. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.015

Raw tables: 176,422 neuron records; 25,862,574 directed edges at weight 1 and above; 6,287,789 at weight 5 and above. Edge weight is the neuPrint ConnectsTo.weight property (synapse count).

Pipeline

The battery is a Python pipeline built on igraph (C-backed, the only practical choice at this scale). Stages, in order:

  1. Graph build. Directed weighted graphs at synapse thresholds 5 and 11 (edges kept when weight is at least the threshold). Self-loops removed after construction: 40 at threshold 5, 11 at threshold 11.
  2. Reachability. Exact sensory-to-motor pair counts via condensation bitsets: 26,972 in-graph sensory neurons by 2,232 in-graph motor neurons, 60,201,504 pairs. Hop-limited fractions and mean shortest paths sampled over 200 sources.
  3. Edge-disjoint paths. Unit-capacity max flow on 200 sampled pairs per threshold.
  4. Minimum cut. Directed s-t min cut between the sensory and motor sets via super source and super sink with infinite-capacity super edges.
  5. Lesions. Node removal (random, out-degree, approximate betweenness, min-cut membership) and edge removal (random, weight) at fractions 1, 2, 5, 10, and 20 percent, tracking newly disconnected pairs; degree-matched random controls via out-degree quantile bins where feasible.
  6. Nulls. Degree-preserving and region-constrained directed rewiring by double-edge swaps, 20 replicates each, weights preserved.
  7. Statistics. Percentile bootstrap confidence intervals, one-sided permutation tests with the +1 correction, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR over 24 tests.

Reproducibility audit

An independent audit (2026-09-13, fresh pandas checks, no pipeline code) confirmed every headline number: edge counts, self-loop counts, node counts, and the reconciliation with the paper below. Reachability, min-cut, lesion, and statistics code was reviewed but not re-implemented from scratch.

Known wart, documented not hidden: the threshold 11 graph is stored with 173,023 vertices including 6,149 isolates, so its headline node count overstates the effective graph, and threshold 11 lesion fractions are computed over a pool that includes isolates (mildly diluting targeted-removal fractions). The atlas shows the effective count 166,874 wherever node counts appear.

What the atlas does not claim

  • No lesion comparison on this site is statistically significant after FDR correction. They are exploratory.
  • Structural routes are not behavior. Nothing here predicts what the fly does.
  • Threshold 0 (the full 25.6M-edge graph) was not run; only thresholds 5 and 11 are shown.
  • Nothing here claims to be the first of anything.
